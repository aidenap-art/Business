1. Who we are

2. What we collect

Information you give us

When you join the waitlist we collect your email address and, if you choose to give them, your name, business name, website or social handle, and monthly marketing budget range. We record the date and time you signed up.

If you contact us or become a client, we'll also hold what you send us and what's needed to deliver the service (for example, access to ad or social accounts you grant us). That's covered by your service agreement as well as this policy.

Information collected automatically

  • IP address: used briefly in our server's memory to stop spam and abuse (rate-limiting). We don't save it with your waitlist entry. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security.
  • Cookie and browser storage: see Cookies below.

We don't knowingly collect sensitive information (such as health, religion or political views). Please don't send it to us.

6. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist information until 12 months after launch, or until you ask us to delete it or unsubscribe, whichever comes first. If you become a client, we keep client records for as long as the law requires (for example, tax records).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • correct it, or delete it;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to it;
  • move it to another service (portability);
  • withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect anything we did before).

Email [privacy@yourdomain.com] and we'll reply within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

California residents: we don't sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to your local data protection authority (for example, the ICO in the UK). We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

8. Security and international transfers

We use HTTPS, restrict who can access the waitlist, and protect exports with a secret access key. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe and will tell you and the regulator about a serious breach where the law requires.

Our providers may process information outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Children

This website isn't aimed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Creators under 18 can only work with us with a parent or guardian's agreement (see our Terms of Service). If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes and contact

We'll update this policy when our practices change and change the “last updated” date above. If the changes are significant, we'll tell waitlist members by email.
